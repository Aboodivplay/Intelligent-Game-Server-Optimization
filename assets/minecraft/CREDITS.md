# Minecraft workspace artwork

The block faces and inventory items come from Pixel Perfection Community Edition, the 16×16 Minecraft Java resource pack originally created by XSSheep and maintained by community contributors. The source revision used here is `28e38cab7c1f03f86364ef704f705bebcc13cb3d`. [Resource pack and contributor credits](https://github.com/Athemis/PixelPerfectionCE/tree/28e38cab7c1f03f86364ef704f705bebcc13cb3d)

The pack uses Creative Commons Attribution–ShareAlike 4.0 International. The stone and oak plank tiles and inventory sprites are copied from the pack. The grass-block icon and the Overworld landscape are adaptations under the same license. The landscape uses an original arrangement of the pack’s grass, leaves, logs, planks, door, stone, snow, sand and water textures, with lighting, biome tints and a reduced color palette.
